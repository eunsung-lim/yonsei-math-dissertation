# Yonsei University Mathematics Dissertation Template

This project provides a LaTeX template for writing your dissertation as a graduate student in the Department of Mathematics at Yonsei University.

## Overview

- This template is structured for mathematical dissertations and should help streamline your writing process.
- There are several `TODO` comments throughout the files to guide you on what details or content you need to add or modify for your own dissertation.
- Features such as *List of Figures*, *List of Tables*, and *Appendix* are **not** included, keeping the template simple and focused.

## How to Use

1. **Clone this repository:**
   ```sh
   git clone <repository-url>
   ```
2. **Edit the files:**
    - Open the template files in your preferred LaTeX editor.
    - Fill in your personal and dissertation information wherever you find a `TODO` comment—for example, enter your name, thesis title, advisor, year, or other relevant details as prompted in the `.tex` files.
    - Add your bibliography by including your references in `reference_thesis.bib` using BibTeX entry format (see existing examples inside the file).
3. **Compile your dissertation:**  
   You can use `pdflatex` or another LaTeX compiler to generate your dissertation PDF.

## Tips

- Follow the comments in each `.tex` file for guidance.
- For references, add BibTeX entries in `reference_thesis.bib`.
